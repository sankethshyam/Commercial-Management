About

A focused commercial practice, built around finding the right fit and carrying the process forward with care.

I am Sanketh Shyam, working in commercial sourcing and procurement. My focus is practical: Understand what a business needs, identify relevant options, assess the commercial details and help move the agreed transaction toward completion.

The work sits at the commercial layer between requirements and delivery—supplier relationships, quotations, terms, clear communication and dependable follow-through. I am building a specialist professional practice grounded in these fundamentals.
